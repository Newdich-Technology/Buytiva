# Buytiva identity concepts

## Explored directions

- Ribbon Pulse: two offset, folded paths meet around a lime diamond. The open center carries the idea of a useful connection and onward movement.
- Connect Loop: two equal rounded arcs join into an even, symmetrical link. Clear but more generic at small sizes.
- Tiv Rhythm: three compact bars rise and fall into a shared beat. Distinctive cadence, but weaker as a standalone brand silhouette.

## Selected: Ribbon Pulse

Ribbon Pulse balances a recognizable outline with a small negative-space bridge. It uses two separated color paths and a central diamond, not a retail object or library icon. It remains a simple silhouette at 16px and can reproduce in one color. This is an internal distinctiveness review, not trademark clearance.

The blue path leads left-to-right into the coral counter-path; the lime center is the connection point. The mark keeps at least one-half of its own width as clear space. The colored variant is intended for light and dark surfaces. Use monochrome exports where color is unavailable. Do not rotate, add outlines, change the path spacing, or use the mark as a cart or location pin.

Wordmark uses a bold geometric sans treatment with tight optical spacing. Web uses Space Grotesk when available with an Arial fallback. The SVG wordmark remains editable text; system rendering can vary slightly by platform.
