# Phase 2 procurement audit

The implemented routes and limitations are in `docs/backend/procurementImplementationStatus.md` and the [master checklist](PHASE_0_TO_2_MASTER_CHECKLIST.md). Addresses, shopping lists, zones, pricing, discovery, requests, versioned quotations and initial payment-gated orders exist. Product must approve threshold, rounding and commission policy; price preview is not binding. Preferred-address selection and retrieval passed disposable PostgreSQL CI 45667707752. Customer display-name update is implemented with new tests pending CI; other profile functionality, expiry jobs, refunds, notification events and dispute resolution remain incomplete.

An accepted quotation could previously be converted to an order after the procurer lost compliance eligibility or the matching zone was disabled. `src/repositories/orders/order.repository.ts` now rechecks the persisted address snapshot, active zone and eligibility in its serializable creation transaction. CI 45665163104 passed PostgreSQL tests checking loss of coverage and compliance without creating an order. This does not stop future eligibility changes after creation; later transitions recheck actor eligibility and payment, and operations still need explicit product policy for post-creation suspension.

Initial private review creation and owner retrieval now exist; see `REVIEW_POLICY.md`. Moderation, public reviews and aggregates do not exist, and dispute/refund/moderation policy remains open. Direct test insertion of a verified payment record is not provider integration and does not support real-money readiness.
